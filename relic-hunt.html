Field notes

Reading the hillshade

  • Straight lines and square corners are people. Nature doesn't make them.
  • A rectangle of low ridges is a house foundation. A round pit beside it is the cellar hole.
  • A long shallow groove is an old wagon road, worn down by wheels and hooves.
  • A zigzag trench with a mound beside it is a Civil War earthwork.
  • Flip to Aerial to see what the woods hide from ordinary photos.

Your detector

  • High beep means silver, like coins, spoons and watches. Copper cents beep high too.
  • Low beep means gold, but brass buttons and lead bullets beep low too.
  • Choppy scratch is aluminum: pop cans, pull tabs and foil. Skip it.
  • Low grunt is iron: nails, bottle caps and wire. Skip that too.

Every swing costs battery. Every hole costs a dig. Don't waste them on empty woods.
